Privacy Policy

SourcingTree (“we”) respects the privacy of everyone who uses our mobile applications (the “Service”). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how you can control it.

1. What we collect and why

There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, phone number, or address. We process only the minimum needed to run the Service.

About the device identifier
We do not store the raw Android ID. We store only an irreversible SHA-256 hash of it, and we use that value solely to manage the free trial. The value alone cannot identify you.

2. How we collect it

  • Generated automatically when you launch the app.
  • Entered by you when you use the contact form.

4. Data that never leaves your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us.

  • Connected vehicles, vehicle nicknames, and auto-lock settings
  • Bluetooth device names and connect/disconnect times
  • Step and heading sensor readings and the distances derived from them
  • Activity history (lock requests and their results)

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information at any time.

  • Account and data deletion: Account & Data Deletion
  • Anything else: contact us using the details below.

We respond within 10 days of receiving your request.

6. Deletion

We delete personal information without delay once its retention period ends or its purpose is met. Electronic files are erased so that they cannot be recovered; any printed material is shredded or incinerated.

7. Security

  • Device identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes, never in raw form.
  • Sensitive values kept on the device are held in encrypted storage.
  • Access to personal information is limited to the minimum number of people.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we delete it immediately.
