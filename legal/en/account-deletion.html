Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete the account created by SourcingTree apps and the data stored with it.

When is an account created?

There is no sign-up in our apps. We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

However, to manage the free trial, an anonymous account is created automatically the first time you launch the app, derived from a one-way hash of your device identifier. That account holds nothing but the time your trial started, and the value alone cannot identify you.

Option 1 · Uninstall the app (data on your device)

Uninstalling removes the following from your device. No request is needed.

  • Connected vehicles and their nicknames
  • Auto-lock settings and Bluetooth device settings
  • Activity history (lock requests and their results)
Uninstalling does not remove the anonymous account described above, because it is kept so that your remaining trial period carries over if you reinstall. Use Option 2 to delete it.

Option 2 · Request account deletion

Email us and we will delete the anonymous account and any data held on our side.

Send a deletion request

Including the following helps us process it faster.

  • The name of the app you use
  • The device information shown at the bottom of the app’s More screen, if available

We complete the request and reply within 10 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

ItemActionNotes
Anonymous account and device identifier hashDeleted immediatelyCannot be recovered.
Free trial recordDeleted immediatelyAfter deletion, reinstalling may start a new trial.
Vehicles, settings and activity history on the deviceRemoved when you uninstallThis data is never sent to us.
Crash data (Crashlytics)Deleted automatically 90 days after collectionHeld in a form that does not identify you.
Purchase and subscription recordsRetained by Google PlayGoogle keeps these as required by law. Cancel a subscription from Google Play.

If you have an active subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Please cancel it first at Google Play subscriptions.
